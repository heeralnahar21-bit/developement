welcome to codehelp

CodeHelp is a highly popular online ed-tech platform and coding community founded by Love Babbar (a prominent software engineer and former SDE at Amazon and Microsoft) alongside Lakshay Kumar. The platform is dedicated to providing high-quality, structured computer science education to help students and working professionals master coding and clear technical placements. CodeHelp offers comprehensive bootcamps and courses covering essential domains like Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), Full-Stack MERN Web Development, and Low-Level Design (LLD). Known for its curated, practical, and beginner-friendly learning paths, the platform operates under an all-in-one learning ecosystem called CodeHelp ONE, which provides students with full course libraries, mock tests, coding contests, and built-in AI support through a unified subscription model.
