CodeHelp is a popular Indian e-learning platform and coding community. It was co-founded by Love Babbar, a prominent software engineer and YouTuber who previously worked at Amazon and Microsoft. The platform aims to bridge the gap between academic education and industry standards by offering practical, to-the-point tutorials on Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), Web Development, and core Computer Science subjects. Alongside highly structured free playlists on YouTube, CodeHelp features premium bootcamps, curated problem-solving sheets (like the famous 450 DSA Sheet), mock tests, and interview preparation guides.
